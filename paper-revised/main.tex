\begin{abstract}

In this paper, we prove a necessary and sufficient condition for Tracy-Widom law of Wigner matrices. Consider $N \times N$ symmetric Wigner matrices $H$ with $H_{ij} = N^{-1/2} x_{ij}$, whose upper right entries $x_{ij}$ $(1\le i< j\le N)$ are $i.i.d.$ random variables with distribution $\mu$ and diagonal entries $x_{ii}$ $(1\le i\le N)$ are $i.i.d.$ random variables with distribution $\wt \mu$. The means of $\mu$ and $\wt \mu$ are zero, the variance of $\mu$ is 1, and the variance of $\wt \mu $ is finite. We prove that, for real symmetric Wigner matrices, $\lim_{s\to \infty}s^4\p(|x_{12}| \ge s)=0$ is necessary and sufficient for the edge universality of the largest eigenvalue $\lambda_N$. If it holds, the distribution function of $N^{2/3}(\lambda_N-2)$ is that of the GOE up to a shift $N^{-\delta}$ and an additive error $o(1)$ uniform in $s$, so that, by the theorem of Tracy and Widom \cite{TR1, TR2}, it converges to the Tracy-Widom law $F_1$. If it fails, $N^{2/3}(\lambda_N-2)$ is not tight. The proof, including the results of \cite{EKYY1}, \cite{EKYY2}, \cite{EYY} that it uses, is formalized in Lean 4 \cite{Lean}; the Tracy-Widom limit of the GOE is taken from \cite{TR1, TR2} and is not part of the formalization.
\end{abstract}
